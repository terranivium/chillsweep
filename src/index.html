ChillSweep checks your user folders against what is actually installed: programs, shortcuts, running apps and Steam games. Then it explains what each item is and what happens if you remove it.

Scanning only reads. Nothing is removed until you choose items and confirm.

    How it works

    A scan only reads your folders and the registry; it never changes anything or starts other programs. When you clean up, items go to the Recycle Bin (and can be undone), except safe-to-clear caches if you choose to delete those permanently.
