ChillSweep

Finds what uninstalled apps, caches, dev projects and games left behind.

ChillSweep checks your user folders against what is actually installed — your apps, what's set to launch, what's running now, and your Steam games. Then it explains what each item is and what happens if you remove it.

Scanning only reads. Nothing is removed until you choose items and confirm.

    How it works

    A scan only reads; it never changes anything or starts other programs.

    When you clean up, items can be put back afterwards, except safe-to-clear caches if you choose to delete those permanently.
